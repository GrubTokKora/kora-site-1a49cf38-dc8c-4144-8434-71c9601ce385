Festival Calendar

Celebrations throughout the year

Shirdi Sai Mandir in Sterling observes the full cycle of Hindu festivals alongside Sai Baba–specific celebrations. From Rama Navami in spring to Diwali in autumn, each event is marked with special poojas, abhishekams, and prasadam for all devotees.

The mandir is open seven days a week — weekdays 8:00 AM–12:30 PM and 6:00 PM–9:00 PM, and weekends 8:00 AM–9:00 PM. Festival days often feature extended hours and additional sevas; please contact us or follow our Facebook page for the latest updates.

Every Week & Every Month

Recurring sevas at the mandir

Beyond the yearly festivals, these sevas take place regularly at Shirdi Sai Mandir in Sterling — giving devotees a consistent opportunity to connect with Baba.

Daily Aartis

Four aartis every day — Kakad (8 AM), Madhyan (12 PM), Dhoop (6 PM), and Shej (8:30 PM) — open to all devotees.

Thursday Sai Pooja

Thursdays are especially sacred to Sai Baba — special bhajans and archana are offered every Thursday evening.

Monthly Abhishekam

Sai Abhishekam is performed monthly and is open for devotee sponsorship. Contact the mandir to schedule your seva.

Satyanarayana Vratam

Monthly Satyanarayana pooja performed at the temple — devotees may also request this at home.

Sandwich Seva

Ongoing community food drive — volunteers gather to prepare and distribute food to those in need in Northern Virginia.

Maha Prasadam Sundays

Select Sundays feature Maha Prasadam for the congregation — check the mandir's Facebook page for the current schedule.
