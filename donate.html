Donate

No amount is small. All donations are tax-deductible and sustain the daily life and future of the Mandir.

Why Give

Your generosity keeps Baba's home alive

Donations support the daily administration of the Mandir, ongoing renovation and maintenance, the construction of the new main building, and community service like Sandwich Seva (a food drive for the homeless). Gifts are accepted as cash, check, debit/credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), and in the form of articles and commodities.

Construction Fund

Help build the permanent Sai Baba Mandir in Northern Virginia. Contributions to the Construction Fund have a suggested minimum of $1,001, and go entirely toward building the main mandir.

Eco-Friendly Clay Ganesha Idols

Shirdi Sai Mandir handcrafts eco-friendly clay Ganesha idols for devotees to bring home for Ganesha puja. Each idol is made at the mandir using only clay and is approximately 9 inches tall.

$27 per idol
