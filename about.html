About Shirdi Sai Mandir

Built by devotion, sustained by seva — a spiritual home in Northern Virginia.

Our Story

From a small beginning to a permanent home

Shirdi Sai Mandir was established as a non-profit organization in 2006 in leased premises, and was initially run entirely by volunteers. In 2008, a priest was hired to perform the daily services to Baba.

In 2012, the community acquired 11 acres of land, and in June 2013 the Mandir moved to its permanent site with an elaborate three-day sthapana ceremony. Today the temple is open seven days a week and all four aartis are offered to Baba.

The existing structures have been renovated to accommodate Baba's Dwarakamayi, and our vision continues — to build a permanent grand Sai Mandir with the blessings of Baba and the support of devotees.

The Sadguru

About Shirdi Sai Baba

Sai Baba, one of the foremost sadgurus of India, lived in Shirdi for 60 years and elevated it to the status of a great spiritual center. He never preached, toured or discoursed, and shunned unnecessary publicity — yet by the sheer brilliance of his spiritual fire he drew innumerable devotees from all over the country, irrespective of caste or creed.

He took Samadhi in 1918, yet his power to mould and develop his devotees remains a dynamic spiritual force. He taught a moral code of love, forgiveness, charity, contentment, inner peace and devotion to God and guru, and always uttered “Allah Malik” (“God is King”).

“Trust in me and your prayer shall be answered.”

We urge readers new to the Sai philosophy to read the Sai Satcharitra — the wonderful life and teachings of the supreme master of our age.
