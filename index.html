About the Mandir

Built by devotion, sustained by seva

Shirdi Sai Mandir began in 2006 as a small volunteer-run temple in leased premises. In 2012 the community acquired 11 acres of land, and in June 2013 the Mandir moved to its permanent home with an elaborate three-day sthapana ceremony.

Today the temple is open seven days a week, all four aartis are offered to Baba, and the vision continues — to build a permanent grand Sai Mandir in Northern Virginia with the blessings of Baba and the support of devotees.
